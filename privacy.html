Privacy Policy

Applies to all Inchkin apps and games. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

The short version

Our apps collect no personal data. They have no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and they make no network connections.

Data on your Mac

Your settings (for example theme, size, sound or timer length) are stored only on your Mac in the app's own sandboxed preferences. They never leave your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Permissions

Some features ask for a macOS permission, and only when you turn them on. Pointling's optional shortcut display uses Input Monitoring to show key combos such as ⌘C on screen while it is active. Keystrokes are never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. You can turn the permission off at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Emails you send us

If you email us, for example through an app's "Send idea" button (which opens your own Mail app; you review and send the message yourself), we receive what you choose to send: your email address, your message and any pictures. We use it only to reply and to improve our apps. If you agree, we may credit your name in the app when we build your idea. We do not sell or share it. We keep emails as long as needed to answer and improve the app; to have yours deleted, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Our apps collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
