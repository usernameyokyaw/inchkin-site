Support

Help for all Inchkin apps and games.

Contact us

Email inchkin.studio@gmail.com. Please name the app, its version (in the app's About window) and your macOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Pointling

  • Where is the app? Pointling lives in the menu bar. If you can't see its icon (the menu bar may be full), open Pointling again from Launchpad or Finder and its settings window appears.
  • Shortcut display doesn't work. It needs Input Monitoring: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Input Monitoring › turn on Pointling. Only key combos like ⌘C are shown; plain typing is never shown or stored.
  • Too loud or too much motion? Mute sounds from the menu. Choose a smaller size or the "clean rings" burst style in Settings.

Wrapglow

  • Where is the app? Wrapglow lives in the menu bar. If you can't see its icon, open Wrapglow again from Launchpad or Finder and its settings window appears. You can also turn on "Show in Dock" in Settings.
  • Will my audience see the glow? No. The glow and the time readout are kept out of screen sharing and screen recordings by macOS, so only you see them. Check once with your meeting app before an important talk.
  • Shortcuts: ⌃⌥T starts or pauses the timer, ⌃⌥R resets it. If another app already uses a shortcut, Wrapglow's menu still works.
  • Too much motion or hard to see? With Reduce Motion on (System Settings › Accessibility › Display) the last-minute pulse stops. With Increase Contrast on, the glow uses stronger colours. You can also make the edge thicker in Settings.
  • No chime? Chimes are off by default; turn them on in Settings. They follow your Mac's volume.
